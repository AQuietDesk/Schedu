# Schedu: refunds and support

Last updated: November 7, 2026

## Price

Schedu is free. On Polar you can pay whatever you like for it, including nothing. Paying unlocks
no extra features. It supports further work on the app.

## Schedu Pro

Schedu Pro is a yearly subscription sold through Polar. It adds syllabus import to Schedu.

- **What you get:** a licence key that unlocks Schedu Pro on up to 3 computers at a time. You
  can move it between computers with **Settings → Schedu Pro → Remove from this PC**.
- **Your own Anthropic account:** syllabus import uses your own Anthropic API key. Anthropic
  bills you directly for each import (usually a few cents), under Anthropic's terms. That cost
  isn't part of the subscription and AQuietDesk can't refund it.
- **Renewal and cancelling:** the subscription renews each year until you cancel, which you can
  do at any time from your Polar purchases page (aquietdesk.com/portal). Pro keeps working until
  the end of the year you paid for. After that, import turns off, but everything you imported
  stays in Schedu and the free app keeps working.
- **Offline use:** Schedu Pro checks your key about once a week and keeps working for 30 days
  without a connection.
- **Check before you save:** imported dates come from your syllabus and are flagged when
  they're missing or unusual, but you're responsible for checking them against the syllabus.
  The review screen shows the source text for every row.

## Refunds

If you paid for Schedu or Schedu Pro and want your money back, email **discover@aquietdesk.com** within
**30 days** of buying, with the email address you used at checkout. You'll get a full refund, and
you don't have to give a reason. You can keep using the free app afterwards. A refunded Schedu
Pro key stops working at its next check.

Payments are processed by Polar (polar.sh), the seller of record, so the refund comes from Polar
to your original payment method. It usually arrives within 5 to 10 business days, depending on
your bank.

## Support

Email **discover@aquietdesk.com**. Support is provided by one person, so allow up to 3 business
days for a reply. It helps to include:

- your Schedu version (shown at the bottom of **Settings**);
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
- a screenshot, if something looks wrong.

Please don't send your backup file or Google password. Support never needs either.
